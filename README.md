### 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a lecturer and researcher in the **Faculty of Computer Science** at the **Vietnam-Korea University of Information and Communication Technology (VKU)**, The University of Da Nang. 

This GitHub is my personal corner for sharing **open-source experiments, developer tools, workshop materials, and AI/mobile engineering projects**.

* 🎓 **Teaching**: Software Engineering, Mobile Cross-Platform Development (Flutter, React Native), Project Management, and Agentic AI Systems.
* 🔬 **Research**: Applied Machine Learning, Computer Vision, Digital Twins, and trustworthy Human–AI collaboration.
* 🛠️ **Building**: Founder & builder exploring agentic workflows, product engineering, and practical AI implementations through initiatives like [AITwinLab.org](https://aitwinlab.org).
